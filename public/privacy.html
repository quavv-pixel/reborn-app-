The short version

  • Without an account, everything you enter stays on your device. We never see it.

What we collect

WhatWhenWhy
Your tracker data (workouts, habits, meals, budget, notes, profiles) Always on your device. In our cloud database only if you create an account. To run the app and sync it between your devices.
Email address and a securely hashed password If you create an account. To sign you in and send password-reset emails.
Payment status (paid / not paid, renewal date) If you buy REBORN Pro. To unlock Pro features. Your card details go straight to Stripe; we never see or store them.
Crash reports (error message, browser type, page address) If the app crashes. To find and fix bugs. Crash reports don't include your tracker data.
Anonymous page-view and speed statistics When you use the app. To see how many people use it and whether it loads fast. No cookies, no tracking across sites.

Profile passwords

Each profile's password is scrambled (hashed) on your device before it's saved. If you use cloud sync, only that scrambled version is uploaded, never the password itself. If you forget a profile password, we can't recover it.

  • Vercel: hosts the website and collects the anonymous statistics.
  • Supabase: hosts the account and sync database ([REGION, e.g. US East]).
  • Stripe: processes payments.
  • Sentry: receives crash reports.

Each handles data under its own privacy policy and only for the job above.
